📊 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI Project
 
Updated — October 2026
Originally built August 2026. Significantly revised and improved — new layout, cleaner design, and full year interactivity.
 
 
 
📌 About This Project
 
I used a public Superstore sales dataset containing orders, products, customers, and regional information. I cleaned the data, built proper relationships between tables, and created an interactive dashboard to explore business performance across four years (2014–2017).
 
 
 
🛠️ What I Did
 
- Loaded and checked all four data files
​
- Cleaned inconsistent formatting and values
​
- Created relationships so the data connected properly
​
- Explored patterns and trends
​
- Designed and built the Power BI dashboard
 
 
 
📈 What the Dashboard Shows
 
At a glance — Key Numbers:
 
- Total Sales: £2,000,000
​
- Total Profit: £283,000
​
- Total Quantity Sold: 37,000 units
 
Breakdown by:
 
- Region — West is our strongest market, followed by East, Central, and South
​
- Category — Technology leads in sales, followed by Office Supplies and Furniture
​
- Profit Margin — Technology and Office Supplies are most profitable; Furniture has the lowest margin
​
- Customer Segment — Consumers are our largest group, then Corporate, then Home Office
​
- Yearly Trend — Sales grew from £481K in 2014 to £725K in 2017 (~51% growth)
​
- Monthly Pattern — Quiet start to the year, steady through summer, strong peak in November and December
 
✨ Interactive Feature:
You can click on any year (2014–2017) and every chart updates instantly. It's really helpful to see how each region, category, and month performed in that specific year.
 
 
 
💡 Key Insights
 
- ✅ Business grew consistently year on year
​
- ✅ Technology is our strongest performer — top sales AND top profit margin
​
- ✅ Furniture sells well but brings in less profit per sale
​
- ✅ Q4 is clearly our most important season — planning ahead really matters here
​
- ✅ The West region consistently outperforms others — worth understanding why

This is a personal portfolio project using publicly available data. It's for learning and demonstration purposes — showing the full process from raw data to interactive insights.
